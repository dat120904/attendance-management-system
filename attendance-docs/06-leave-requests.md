# 06. Leave Requests

## Chuc nang

- Tao don xin nghi.
- Chon loai nghi: phep nam, nghi om, nghi khong luong, nghi bu.
- Kiem tra so ngay phep con lai.
- Dinh kem tai lieu.
- Luong phe duyet theo quan ly truc tiep va HR.
- Huy don neu chua duyet.
- Dong bo trang thai nghi vao Attendance Logs.

## Trang thai don

- `Draft`
- `Pending Manager`
- `Pending HR`
- `Approved`
- `Rejected`
- `Cancelled`

## Luong phe duyet

1. Employee tao don.
2. He thong kiem tra so du phep va trung lich.
3. Manager duyet hoac tu choi.
4. HR duyet buoc cuoi neu chinh sach yeu cau.
5. He thong tru phep va cap nhat lich nghi.
6. Attendance Logs hien `On Leave`.

## Quyen truy cap

- Employee: tao, xem trang thai, huy neu chua vao quy trinh duyet.
- Manager: duyet hoac tu choi don cua cap duoi.
- HR: kiem tra quy phep, duyet HR, cap nhat so du phep.
- Payroll: xem don da duyet de tinh cong.
- Admin: can thiep va cau hinh quy trinh.

## Test can co

- Tao don nghi thanh cong.
- Khong tao duoc don vuot so du phep neu chinh sach khong cho.
- Manager chi duyet duoc don cap duoi.
- HR cap nhat leave balance dung.
- Don bi tu choi khong tru phep.
- Don da duyet hien tren Attendance Logs.
