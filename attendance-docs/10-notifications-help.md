# 10. Thong bao va Help Center

## Chuc nang thong bao

- Thong bao don nghi duoc duyet/tu choi.
- Thong bao log cham cong bat thuong.
- Thong bao sap het gio check-out.
- Thong bao yeu cau dieu chinh cong.
- Thong bao ky cong can xac nhan.

## Kenh thong bao

- In-app notification.
- Email.
- Slack/Teams neu tich hop sau.

## Help Center

- Trang cau hoi thuong gap.
- Huong dan check-in/check-out.
- Huong dan tao don nghi.
- Huong dan dieu chinh cong.
- Gui yeu cau ho tro.

## Quyen truy cap

- Employee: xem thong bao cua minh, gui yeu cau ho tro.
- Manager: xem thong bao lien quan doi nhom.
- HR: xem thong bao HR, xu ly yeu cau cham cong/nghi phep.
- Payroll: xem thong bao lien quan ky cong.
- Admin: cau hinh mau thong bao va danh muc Help Center.

## Test can co

- Tao notification dung nguoi nhan.
- Mark as read hoat dong dung.
- Khong xem duoc notification cua nguoi khac.
- Email retry khi gui loi.
- Help Center search dung bai viet.
