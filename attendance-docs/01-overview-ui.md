# 01. Muc tieu va thiet ke giao dien ban dau

## Muc tieu san pham

Xay dung ung dung cham cong noi bo cho doanh nghiep, bat dau tu man hinh Dashboard trong anh dinh kem. Ung dung can ho tro nhan vien tu cham cong, theo doi gio lam, xin nghi phep; quan ly va HR co the duyet, dieu chinh, xuat bao cao va tong hop du lieu tinh luong.

## Bo cuc tong the

- Sidebar ben trai nen xanh den, rong khoang 320px tren desktop.
- Header tren cung nen trang, gom tieu de trang, icon thong bao, icon ung dung, nut `Check In` va avatar nguoi dung.
- Noi dung chinh nen xam rat nhat, chia thanh vung dashboard lon ben trai va cac the thong tin nhanh ben phai.
- Su dung card nen trang, vien xam nhat, bo goc nhe, khoang cach rong rai.
- Mau nhan dien chinh: xanh duong dam cho nut hanh dong, icon va thanh tien do.

## Sidebar

Ten ung dung:

- `Workforce Pro`
- Mo ta tai khoan: `Enterprise Admin`

Menu chinh:

- Dashboard
- Attendance Logs
- Leave Requests
- Payroll Summaries
- Settings

Menu phu:

- Help Center
- Logout

Trang dang active can co nen xanh dam hon va vach xanh o mep trai.

## Header

- Tieu de trang hien tai, vi du `Dashboard`.
- Icon thong bao.
- Icon menu ung dung.
- Nut nhanh `Check In` hoac `Check Out` tuy trang thai hien tai.
- Avatar nguoi dung.

## Dashboard ban dau theo anh

Khoi chao mung:

- Loi chao theo thoi gian: `Good morning, Alex.`
- Dong phu: `Here is your timekeeping summary for today, Thursday, October 26th.`

The phien cham cong hien tai:

- Nhan: `CURRENT SESSION`
- Dong ho dem thoi gian dang lam: `03:45:15`
- Gio check-in: `Checked in at 08:30 AM`
- Nut hanh dong lon: `Check Out`

Cum KPI ben phai:

- `Hours this week`: hien thi `32.5 / 40h`, thanh tien do.
- `Remaining leave`: hien thi so ngay nghi con lai.
- `Next holiday`: hien thi ten ngay le va khoang ngay.

Bang `Recent Logs`:

- Cot: Date, Check-in, Check-out, Total hours, Status.
- Trang thai `On Time`: xanh la nhat.
- Trang thai `Late`: vang nhat.
- Trang thai `On Leave`: xam.
- Link `View All` dan den trang Attendance Logs.

## Responsive

- Desktop: sidebar co dinh, noi dung chia 2 cot.
- Tablet: sidebar thu gon thanh icon, dashboard va KPI co the xep 1 cot.
- Mobile: sidebar thanh drawer, nut cham cong noi bat o tren, bang log chuyen sang card theo ngay.
