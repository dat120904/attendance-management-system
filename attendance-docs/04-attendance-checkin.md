# 04. Cham cong Check-in / Check-out

## Chuc nang

- Check in.
- Check out.
- Dem thoi gian current session.
- Ghi nhan vi tri, thiet bi, IP neu cong ty bat cau hinh.
- Cho phep dinh kem anh hoac selfie neu can.
- Xu ly truong hop quen check-out.
- Chan check-in trung lap khi dang co phien mo.
- Hien thi trang thai trong ngay: chua check-in, dang lam, da check-out, thieu du lieu.

## Luong nghiep vu co ban

1. Nhan vien bam `Check In`.
2. He thong kiem tra phien cham cong dang mo.
3. He thong ghi nhan thoi gian, ngay, user, thiet bi, IP, vi tri neu co.
4. Dashboard doi nut thanh `Check Out`.
5. Dong ho current session bat dau dem.
6. Nhan vien bam `Check Out`.
7. He thong tinh tong gio va cap nhat attendance log.

## Xu ly ngoai le

- Check-out khi chua check-in: bao loi.
- Check-in khi dang co phien mo: bao loi hoac dieu huong ve phien hien tai.
- Quen check-out: tao canh bao va cho tao yeu cau dieu chinh.
- Check-in ngoai vi tri hop le: chan hoac ghi nhan canh bao tuy cau hinh.
- Mat ket noi: hien thi retry va khong tao ban ghi trung.

## Quyen truy cap

- Employee: tao check-in/check-out cua minh.
- Manager: tao cua minh, xem trang thai cap duoi.
- HR: tao cua minh, xem/xu ly ngoai le toan cong ty.
- Payroll: tao cua minh, xem du lieu da tong hop.
- Admin: toan quyen va cau hinh quy tac.

## Test can co

- Check-in thanh cong.
- Check-out thanh cong.
- Tinh tong gio dung.
- Khong cho check-in trung.
- Khong cho check-out khi chua check-in.
- Dong ho session chay lien tuc.
- Loi API hien toast/error state.
- Retry khong tao duplicate.
