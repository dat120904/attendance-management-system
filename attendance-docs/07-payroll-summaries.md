# 07. Payroll Summaries

## Chuc nang

- Tong hop cong theo ky luong.
- Tinh tong gio lam, gio tang ca, gio nghi phep, gio thieu.
- Khoa bang cong sau khi xac nhan.
- Xuat bao cao cho he thong tinh luong.
- Luu lich su phien ban bang cong.
- Canh bao du lieu thieu truoc khi khoa ky.

## Chi so can tinh

- Standard hours.
- Worked hours.
- Overtime hours.
- Paid leave hours.
- Unpaid leave hours.
- Late count.
- Early leave count.
- Missing log count.
- Payroll status.

## Quyen truy cap

- Employee: khong truy cap mac dinh, co the xem phieu cong ca nhan neu doanh nghiep cho phep.
- Manager: xem tong hop doi nhom de doi chieu.
- HR: xac nhan cong, nghi phep, tang ca.
- Payroll: tao ky, tinh toan, khoa ky, xuat file.
- Admin: cau hinh va mo khoa ky khi can.

## Test can co

- Tao ky cong thanh cong.
- Tong hop gio lam dung.
- Tinh nghi phep co luong/khong luong dung.
- Khong khoa ky neu con log thieu bat buoc xu ly.
- Ky da khoa khong bi sua attendance log tuy tien.
- Export dung format.
