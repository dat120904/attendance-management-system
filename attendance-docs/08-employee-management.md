# 08. Quan ly nhan vien

## Chuc nang

- Danh sach nhan vien.
- Tao/sua/khoa tai khoan.
- Gan phong ban.
- Gan chuc danh.
- Gan quan ly truc tiep.
- Gan lich lam viec va chinh sach cham cong.
- Xem lich su cham cong cua tung nhan vien.
- Import/export nhan vien hang loat.

## Du lieu ho so can co

- Ma nhan vien.
- Ho ten.
- Email.
- So dien thoai.
- Phong ban.
- Chuc danh.
- Quan ly truc tiep.
- Ngay vao lam.
- Trang thai lam viec.
- Lich lam viec.
- Chinh sach nghi phep.

## Quyen truy cap

- Employee: khong truy cap danh sach nhan vien.
- Manager: xem nhan vien thuoc doi nhom minh.
- HR: tao/sua/khoa tai khoan nhan vien va gan thong tin HR.
- Payroll: xem thong tin lien quan tinh cong/ky luong.
- Admin: toan quyen.

## Test can co

- HR tao nhan vien thanh cong.
- Manager chi xem nhan vien trong doi nhom.
- Employee khong truy cap duoc danh sach nhan vien.
- Gan lich lam viec anh huong dung den cham cong.
- Khoa tai khoan chan dang nhap.
- Import file validate loi tung dong.
