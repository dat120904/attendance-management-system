- [03-dashboard.md](attendance-docs/03-dashboard.md): dashboard nhan vien, manager, HR/Admin va Payroll.
- [04-attendance-checkin.md](attendance-docs/04-attendance-checkin.md): check-in, check-out, phien cham cong, xu ly ngoai le.
- [05-attendance-logs.md](attendance-docs/05-attendance-logs.md): danh sach cong, loc, chi tiet, dieu chinh cong.
- [06-leave-requests.md](attendance-docs/06-leave-requests.md): xin nghi phep, phe duyet, so du phep.
- [07-payroll-summaries.md](attendance-docs/07-payroll-summaries.md): tong hop cong, ky luong, khoa ky, xuat bao cao.

- [09-settings.md](attendance-docs/09-settings.md): cau hinh he thong, chinh sach, vai tro, bao mat.
- [10-notifications-help.md](attendance-docs/10-notifications-help.md): thong bao va help center.
- [11-permissions.md](attendance-docs/11-permissions.md): phan quyen theo vai tro va theo tung trang.
- [12-data-api.md](attendance-docs/12-data-api.md): bang du lieu va API can co.
- [13-test-plan.md](attendance-docs/13-test-plan.md): ke hoach test tu unit den acceptance.
- [14-roadmap-dod.md](attendance-docs/14-roadmap-dod.md): roadmap theo giai doan, thu tu uu tien va Definition of Done.
